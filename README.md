Turon: A beloved snack made from saba bananas coated in brown sugar and wrapped in lumpia skin, then deep-fried. It's a sweet and crunchy treat often sold as street food.

Fish Balls and Squid Balls: Commonly found in street food stalls, these savory snacks are typically served with a variety of dipping sauces and are perfect for a quick bite on the go.

Chips and Finger Foods: Classic snacks like Chippy, Boy Bawang (fried corn), and Cheese Rings are popular among Filipinos. These snacks are often shared during school breaks or enjoyed while studying. 
1
Empanadas: These are pastry pockets filled with various ingredients, often including meat and vegetables. They are a popular snack option, especially in areas like Gateway Mall. 
1
Dried Mangoes: A favorite among locals and tourists alike, these sweet treats are made from sun-dried mangoes and are often enjoyed as a healthy snack or gift.
